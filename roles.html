Role 01 · Design & Brand

Visual Experience Intern

You'll help define how Crikle looks, feels and communicates — across the product, the socials, and everything in between. If you can't stand seeing a brand look inconsistent across two posts, this is your role.

Apply for this role

What you'll work on

  • Social media creatives, from single posts to full campaign sets
  • Brand identity work — extending the logo, colour and type system you see on this site
  • Product and UI design for the Crikle app
  • Marketing creatives and campaign concepts
  • Presentations and visual assets for partners, press and investors
  • Open-ended creative experiments — the ones nobody's briefed you on yet
Role 02 · Growth

Growth & Community Intern

You'll help figure out how Crikle gets its first 100 users, then 1,000, then 10,000 — and, harder, how it gets them to come back. Equal parts strategist and person willing to DM 200 people in a weekend.

Apply for this role

What you'll work on

  • Growth experiments — testing cheap, fast, reversible ideas
  • Social media strategy across the channels students actually use
  • Student and community acquisition, campus by campus
  • Content strategy and campaign planning
  • Partnerships with clubs, societies and student communities
  • User acquisition and, eventually, retention
Role 03 · Comms

Brand Communications & Outreach Intern

You'll help make people know Crikle exists — and give them a reason to care. If you're the person who already knows which societies, creators and college pages matter, this is built for you.

Apply for this role

What you'll work on

  • Publicity campaigns around key launch moments
  • College outreach — building relationships campus by campus
  • Influencer and creator outreach
  • PR initiatives and media relationships
  • Partnerships and community collaborations
  • Social media communication and launch campaigns

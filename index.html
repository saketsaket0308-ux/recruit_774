Why this, why now

You won't be completing tasks. You'll be deciding what Crikle even is.

Most internships hand you a ticket queue on someone else's finished product. This one hands you a blank canvas six weeks before launch — and asks what you'd do with it.

Work with the founders directly

No layers, no account managers — your ideas reach Saket and Sunand the same day you have them.

Own outcomes, not tasks

A metric, a channel, a look-and-feel — you'll be the person accountable for it, not the person executing someone else's brief.

A portfolio with real stakes

Campaigns that ran, growth loops that worked (or didn't), a product that shipped — proof of work, not a certificate template.

Zero-to-one, up close

Watch — and shape — every early decision: positioning, first users, what breaks, what sticks.

Room to run experiments

Try the campaign nobody's approved a budget for. If it's cheap and reversible, we'd rather learn from it than debate it.

A completion certificate, on delivery

Finish your stint and we'll back it with a certificate and a reference — for work you can actually point to.

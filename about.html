The company

LinkedIn was built for a workforce that doesn't look like yours.

Crikle is a professional network built from scratch for students, creators, founders and young professionals — people whose careers don't fit neatly into "10 years at one company" profiles yet, and shouldn't have to pretend they do.

The idea

Think LinkedIn, reimagined for the next generation.

We're building a place where students, creators, professionals, founders and ambitious young minds can discover people, build a real identity, share opportunities and make professional connections that don't feel like filling out a corporate form.

It's early. There's no polished app to show you yet — the website itself is still coming soon. What exists is a clear point of view about what's broken in how young people network today, and a founding team assembling to go fix it.

Who does well here

We're looking for a specific kind of person.

Not a résumé line — a way of working. If this describes you more than it doesn't, you'll like it here.

✦

High-agency, not high-instruction

You'd rather ship a rough version and fix it than wait for a perfect brief. We'll give you a goal, not a step-by-step.

✦

Comfortable with "we don't know yet"

Early-stage means unresolved questions everywhere. You find that energising, not stressful.

✦

Opinionated, but not precious

You'll fight for an idea — and drop it fast when the data or a better idea shows up.

✦

Already building something, somewhere

A page, a club, a side project, a following — evidence you make things happen without being asked.
